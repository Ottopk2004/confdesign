Privacy Policy for Conf Design

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to you if you register for, log in to or otherwise use a conference website on the Conf Design platform (confdesign.com), whether as a participant, faculty member (speaker), observer, guest or organiser. It describes how your personal data is processed, for which purposes, and which rights you have.

1. Data controller, data processor and contact details

Data controller. The data controller is the organiser of the conference you register for or are logged in to. The organiser decides which information is collected and what it is used for. The organiser's name and email address can be found under "Contact" on the conference website.

Data processor. The Conf Design platform (the website and its data storage) is operated by Otto Poulsen Kragh and Lukas Kvisgaard Andersen, who process personal data on behalf of the organiser and in accordance with the organiser's instructions. This is set out in a data processing agreement that every organiser accepts when creating a conference.

The organiser's responsibility. As data controller, the organiser is responsible for having a lawful basis for the processing, for the information it asks participants to provide, and for all content that the organiser or its faculty upload to the conference website. This includes ensuring that presentations, photos and other files may lawfully be shared, e.g. images of people or material protected by copyright. The platform operators only provide and run the website and do not review uploaded content.

Platform contact: lukaskvisgaard@gmail.com, ottokraghcph@gmail.com

2. Description of the processing

To give you a clear and transparent overview, this section is divided into purposes, categories of personal data, sources, legal basis, recipients and retention.

Purposes

  • Registration: handling and approving your registration, assigning you a participant number and sending it to you by email.
  • Participation in the conference: giving you access to the programme, materials, quizzes and evaluations, and placing you in levels and breakout groups.
  • Certificates: issuing certificates of attendance and documenting participation.
  • Quality assurance: calculating quiz results, collecting evaluations and improving the conference.
  • Practical communication: contacting you about practical matters relating to the conference.
  • Operation and security: running the website securely and troubleshooting errors.

Your personal data is not used for marketing purposes, and it is never sold or rented out.

Categories of personal data

The personal data processed depends on the conference and on what you choose to fill in.

We do not ask for sensitive personal data (e.g. health, religion or trade union membership) or national identification numbers (CPR numbers). Please do not include such information in free-text fields or in an attached PDF. If an attachment contains such information, remove it before uploading.

Sources

  • Directly from you (the registration form, quiz answers, evaluations and uploads)
  • The organiser (processing of your registration, group placement, faculty profiles and materials)
  • Faculty (evaluations of participants and uploaded materials)
  • The platform itself (participant numbers and group placement)
  • Automatically via our hosting provider (technical data)

Legal basis

Personal data is processed on the following legal bases under Article 6 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

Recipients

Within the platform, your personal data is visible to the following:

  • The organiser can see all information about registrants, including any attached PDF, and can export it as a spreadsheet.
  • Faculty can see the participant number and name of participants in the breakout groups they are assigned to.
  • Other participants can see the participant number and name of the people in their own breakout group. Your name appears in group lists once it is registered to your participant number.
  • Anyone with access to the conference website (including guests at open conferences) can see information the organiser has made public, e.g. faculty profiles and uploaded presentations.
  • Your email address, phone number and attached PDF are never shown to other participants or to faculty.

The platform uses the following suppliers, which process data on behalf of the organiser:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. – hosting of the website and storage of data and files (including attached PDFs and presentations). Data may be processed outside the EU/EEA. Any such transfer is based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or another valid transfer mechanism.
  • Resend – sending of emails (approvals with participant number, certificates and quiz results). Your name and email address are processed for this purpose. Transfers to third countries are based on the same mechanisms as above.

Apart from this, personal data is only disclosed to others, such as public authorities, where we are legally required to do so.

Retention

Personal data is not kept longer than necessary. The following retention periods apply to all data on a conference website, including registrations, attached PDFs, quiz and evaluation results, certificate documentation, faculty profiles and uploaded materials:

WhenWhat happens
3 months after the conference endsThe conference website is deactivated. Participants, faculty and guests can no longer log in or access its content.
1 year after the conference endsThe conference and all associated personal data and files are permanently deleted.

The organiser can delete individual registrations, attached PDFs or the entire conference earlier at any time.

3. Mandatory information and automated decisions

Your name and email address are necessary for you to register and to receive your participant number. If you do not provide them, you cannot register. All other fields are marked as optional or required in the form. Whether or not you fill in an optional field has no effect on your registration.

Quizzes are graded automatically, and the result (pass / fail) is calculated against a threshold set by the organiser. The result is used to issue certificates and to evaluate the effectiveness of the teaching. If the result matters to you, you can ask the organiser to have it reviewed by a person. Registrations are approved by the organiser, not automatically.

4. Cookies and browser storage

The website does not use marketing or tracking cookies. To function, the website stores a small amount of information locally in your browser (localStorage): that you have accepted this privacy policy, a random registration ID so you can check the status of your registration, and, if you choose "Remember me", your login to the conference. Organisers may also have a draft of a conference stored locally. This information is not sent to third parties and is deleted when you log out or clear your browser data.

5. Security

The connection to the website is encrypted (HTTPS). Data is stored with Cloudflare, and access to information about registrants is limited to the organiser, who logs in with a PIN code. Attached PDFs can only be downloaded by the organiser.

Participants log in with their personal participant number. Do not share your number with others, and log out if you use a shared computer or tablet. If you suspect that someone has used your number, contact the organiser.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • request access to the personal data processed about you and receive a copy
  • have inaccurate personal data rectified
  • have your personal data erased when there is no longer a basis for keeping it
  • have the processing of your personal data restricted in certain cases
  • receive the personal data you have provided yourself in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format (data portability)
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where processing is based on consent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), datatilsynet.dk

To exercise your rights, contact the organiser (see section 1). You will normally receive a reply within one month.

These rights may be subject to conditions or limitations. Whether, for example, you have a right to data portability depends on the specific circumstances of the processing.

7. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this privacy policy, e.g. if the platform gets new features. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
